# Output format

Put findings first, ordered by severity. Every confirmed finding must include exact file/line references and a concise explanation of impact. Distinguish confirmed findings, open questions/assumptions, optional suggestions, and residual risks.

For audit-flow synthesis, a finding is not confirmed until at least two independent reviewer agents have directly verified the target evidence. Reviewer reports should make provenance clear: identify which primary findings you independently verified, which findings are new/peer-only and therefore need another verifier, and which findings you dispute or could not verify.
