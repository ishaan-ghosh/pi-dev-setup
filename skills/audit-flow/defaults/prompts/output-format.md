# Output format

Put findings first, ordered by severity. Every confirmed finding must include exact file/line references and a concise explanation of impact. Distinguish confirmed findings, open questions/assumptions, optional suggestions, and residual risks.

For audit-flow synthesis, a finding is not confirmed until at least two reviewer agents have separately inspected its target evidence. In a raw peer report, state only the findings and evidence you found from the target; the parent orchestrator maps overlaps and concrete reviewer keys after the blind report is recorded.
